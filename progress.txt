*   `script-src 'self' 'unsafe-eval';` (Often needed for Next.js in dev, but aim to remove for production if possible).

**Edge Cases & Gotchas:**

*   **External Services:** The exact domains for Google Fonts, image hosting, and the AI chat APIs (Groq/OpenRouter) must be correctly identified and allowlisted in the CSP.
